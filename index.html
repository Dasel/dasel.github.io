About

I'm a software developer who builds interactive, real time applications: games, XR experiences and the tools behind them. Over seven years as a professional I've worked on every layer, from engine source code and rendering to gameplay, UI, networking, backends and build pipelines, and I care as much about clean architecture and performance as about how the result feels in someone's hands. I've shipped on PC, mobile, the web and headsets, in startups, research labs and at Unity Technologies, and I'm most at home where a hard technical problem meets a real person using the result.
