About

I started making games as an enthusiast and ended up making them for a living. Over seven years as a professional I've worked on every layer of interactive software, from engine source code and rendering to gameplay, UI, networking and build pipelines, at startups, research labs, Unity Technologies and now Globant. What I enjoy most is the moment a hard technical problem turns into something a real person picks up and plays, whether that's a VR exergame for stroke recovery or a workshop full of students putting on a headset for the first time.
